About Our School

Ahantaman Senior High School, now known as Ahantaman Girls' Senior High School, is located at Ketan in Sekondi, in Ghana's Western Region. The school has served students under several names, including Royal Commercial College and Ahantaman Secondary School.

The school became a girls' senior high school in 2010. This student project uses the name Ahantaman Senior High School as requested.

A brief history

  • Founded in 1948 as Takoradi Commercial Academy.
  • Later known as Royal Commercial College and Royal Commercial Secondary School.
  • The name Ahantaman Secondary School was adopted in 1980.
  • The school became a girls' senior high school in 2010.

School Activities

Learning beyond the classroom

School activities can help learners practise leadership, creativity, teamwork, and service to the community.

Ahantaman students have taken part in science and environmental projects. For example, the school partnered with a science organisation on a student rescue-robot project.

The project aimed to help students explore science, technology, engineering, and mathematics through practical problem-solving. In 2024, the school also worked with the Ghana Ports and Harbours Authority on a tree-planting activity for World Environment Day.

  • Science and technology: hands-on projects that build creativity and problem-solving.
  • Environmental action: caring for the environment through community activities.
  • Student leadership: practising responsibility, collaboration, and service.
  • Culture and recreation: opportunities to share interests and build school spirit.
